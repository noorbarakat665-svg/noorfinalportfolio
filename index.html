02 / About

Behind
the words

I'm a linguist, translator, writer, and editor with an academic background in linguistics and translation. My work spans English–Arabic translation, editorial writing, and language-focused research, informed by a strong interest in how meaning is communicated across cultures and media. I approach each project with attention to linguistic accuracy, clarity, and the needs of its intended audience.
