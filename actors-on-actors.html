Translator’s Note

Subtitling this interview involved transferring spontaneous English conversation into readable Arabic while maintaining the informal, conversational tone of the exchange.

Unlike scripted dialogue, interviews can contain interruptions, repetitions, incomplete sentences, and shifts in emphasis. The main consideration was how to represent natural speech without making the Arabic subtitles unnecessarily wordy or overly formal.

The project also required attention to subtitle segmentation, reading time, and the relationship between spoken dialogue and visual context.

This is an independent academic subtitling exercise, not an official Variety localization.
